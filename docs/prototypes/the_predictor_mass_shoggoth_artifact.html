THE PREDICTOR: MASS

an orbital escape roguelike where prediction, ecology, and appetite make law

First principle: prediction is permission. Omega cannot fire at an unannounced future; the Mass does not need permission at all. Your job is to steal three old counterlaws, wake the east Ascent Gate, and decide whether to outrun the red god or briefly become its favourite small animal.

field brief

  • Escape the Mass. The red substrate expands every turn-cycle. Touch it and you are consumed — unless it has accepted you as temporary tissue.
  • Make the grand crossing. Door-nodes contain compact legacy chambers. Recover 3 shards, open the eastern Ascent Gate, and leave the station before it becomes one answer.
  • Choose your monster. Disagree with Omega, or feed the shoggoth Mass by waiting beside it. Favor lets you cross red tissue and makes it chew nearby warrants.
