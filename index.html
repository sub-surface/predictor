FIELD MANUAL

CASE FILE: ARI VALE

Ari Vale came to the lower station after the evacuation lottery named her impossible: too useful to lose, too erratic to trust, too legible when frightened. Dr. Silas Venn built Omega to show its work: every route, every denial, every locked door with a visible reason. The city learned to hate reasons. IONA kept the archive while Ari descended through rooms that made prediction physical. By the tenth chamber, Omega no longer sounded like a system. It sounded like someone who had read every draft of Ari and wanted the final version to stop moving.

WHAT IS HAPPENING

every unit that hunts you maintains a model of your behavior. the prediction stain marks the tile it predicts you will occupy next; intensity is confidence. stand where it expected, inside its zap range, and you take a hit. bump a unit from a vector it did not predict and it dies. predicted strikes are parried.

THE CORE

Ω THE PREDICTOR is persistent. it trains on every move you make, in every run, forever — death does not reset it. H hive units and the floor-10 avatar read directly from it. its lifetime accuracy on you is shown in the panel and on every death screen.

READOUTS

LEG% — how often recent predictions about you were right. high legibility gets you zapped, but ≡ trust vaults only open above 60%: friends need to be able to model you too. THE EYE ◉/○ — whether this floor is monitored. ▣ caches belong to someone; the game keeps separate ledgers of what you take when watched and unwatched. nothing stops you. something counts.

PROTOCOLS

Between chambers, you can override the station parameters by choosing a PROTOCOL. These are free exploits that modify the Predictor's model, the map geometry, or your own hull integrity.

STRUCTURES

♦ pact shrines — commit to three public moves for a reward; while committed, everything reads you perfectly. THE WARDEN (floor 5) — two containers, filled before you arrived, from its model of your restraint. your choice is remembered for next time.

ENDINGS

kill the avatar on floor 10. how you got there decides what you became: STATIC, THE LONG CON, MUTUAL, or SURVIVOR.
